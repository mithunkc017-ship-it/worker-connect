OVERVIEW Workonnect : 
     Is a smart mobile application designed to connect skilled workers with customers who require various household and professional services. The application provides a secure, user-friendly, and reliable platform where workers such as electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, drivers, cleaners, and other skilled professionals can register, create their profiles, showcase their skills and experience, and receive job opportunities. At the same time, customers can easily search for nearby workers based on their required service, location, experience, ratings, and availability. The application includes three main modules: Worker Module, Customer Module, and Admin Module. Workers can manage their profiles, update their availability, accept or reject job requests, and track their earnings. Customers can search and hire skilled workers, book services, make online payments, and provide ratings and reviews after the service is completed. The Admin module ensures the smooth functioning of the platform by verifying worker profiles, managing users, monitoring activities, handling complaints, and generating reports. The primary objective of Workonnect is to simplify the process of finding skilled professionals, create more employment opportunities, reduce the time and effort required to hire reliable workers, and build trust between workers and customers. By integrating features such as GPSbased worker search, secure authentication, online booking, and digital payments, Workonnect offers a practical and scalable solution to real-world service needs. Workonnect is a modern service marketplace that benefits both workers and customers by making service booking faster, safer, and more convenient while supporting local skilled professionals and promoting employment opportunities.
